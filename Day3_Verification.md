## Reflection

The claim that looked most authoritative but was actually weakest was claim #1: 'The average B.Tech placement package in Indian engineering colleges in 2025 was ₹6.2 LPA (NASSCOM)'. Gemini cited NASSCOM confidently, and Perplexity initially confirmed it using generic news articles. But when I opened the primary NASSCOM source website and reports, I found that NASSCOM does not track or publish B.Tech college placement average packages (as they focus on aggregate IT industry revenue growth and macro-level IT employment trends). The lesson: confidence does not equal correctness. The verification step belongs to the human — every time.
